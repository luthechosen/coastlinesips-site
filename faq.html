Before you book

Answers to the questions we hear most. Don't see yours? Email us and we'll help.

What is Coastline Sips?

Coastline Sips is a mobile mocktail bar bringing an elevated, coastal-inspired beverage experience to weddings, celebrations, private parties, showers, corporate events, and special occasions.

Do you provide alcohol?

Due to Connecticut licensing regulations, we can't provide or sell alcohol. We serve alcohol supplied by you, the host.

When you book, we'll send a shopping guide and calculate how much to buy based on your hours of service and expected guests. Buying your own alcohol can also save you a lot of money.

What drinks do you serve?

We offer an extensive drink menu to choose your signature drinks from. If you'd like something that isn't on the menu, we'll work with you to craft a drink that's unique to your event.

What is your payment policy?

A 50% deposit secures your date on our calendar. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your event.

Can I add more time or another bartender?

Yes. Additional service hours are $225 each, and an additional bartender is $250. See all packages.

Are you insured?

Yes. Liability insurance is included in every package.

Share your date, guest count, and the feel you're going for. We'll reply with availability and a custom quote.
